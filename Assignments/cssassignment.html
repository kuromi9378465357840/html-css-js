Manali

Manali is one of the most beautiful tourist places in India. It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, rivers and peaceful environment.

Many tourists visit Manali every year to enjoy activities such as trekking, skiing, river rafting and camping. It is also a popular destination for people who love nature and adventure.

The combination of mountains, greenery and pleasant weather makes Manali a perfect holiday destination.
