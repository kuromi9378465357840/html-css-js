KIET Deemed To Be University Scholarship Portal

Empowering students to achieve their academic dreams

Scholarship Categories

  • Merit-Based Scholarship: For students with exceptional academic performance (above 90% aggregate).
  • Need-Based Financial Aid: For students facing financial constraints.
  • Sports Excellence Scholarship: For state or national-level sports achievers.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be enrolled or accepted as a full-time student at the university.
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.5 or equivalent percentage for merit categories.
  • Family annual income must be below specified limits for need-based categories.

Required Documents

  • Latest Academic Transcripts / Mark sheets
  • Proof of Family Income (for need-based aid)
  • Valid ID Proof (Aadhaar / Passport / Student ID)
  • Recommendation Letter from a faculty member
